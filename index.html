10. Project Method & Business Use

Problem

Customers may not clearly understand the difference between regular monthly investment and annual lump-sum investment.

Solution

An interactive dashboard provides calculations, comparisons, charts and scenario analysis.

Analysis Used

Mathematical calculation, compound-growth modelling, sensitivity analysis, scenario analysis and data visualization.

Potential Benefit

Supports customer education, staff explanation and awareness of regular investment options.
